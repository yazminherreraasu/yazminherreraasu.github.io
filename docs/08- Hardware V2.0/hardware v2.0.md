## Version 2.0
If I were to create a version 2.0 of my hardware, I would improve a few things after having printed, solder, and test it. My individual subsystem PCB was designed to meet the requirements of the assignment. However, after being able to build my PCB, I started to reflect on what I would do differently if I had to re design it. To start, I would have taken more advantage of the silkscreen layer to label my initials, headers, what pins are ground on the 5V plane, and team name. I believe I could have made the board a bit smaller and more compact and be able to still fit all the components used. This would help me practice to keep in mind the overall product design and actually be able to integrate it to a device prototype. There are more improvements I'd take into account. I would add more test points through out the board to be able take measurements of the power supply, ground, and DAC output more easily. I would also add the option for the user to select the intensity of the speaker output by adding a potentiometer along with it's proper circuitry. I would add a pull down resistor to ground for Logan's trigger input. This is a major change I'd make because this would help prevent the input from floating when it's not connected to Logan's board. By not having included this, my speaker was being activated constantly even after pressing my speaker off button. The proposed improvements are all based on my experience building and testing my PCB board. The overall goal would be that these improvements would produce a more compact, user friendly subsystem.
